Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026

This policy covers the Catford Labs website at www.catfordlabs.com and email you send to our support address. Each of our apps has its own policy, linked below.

Who we are

Catford Labs makes games and apps. For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights, email support@catfordlabs.com.

What this website collects

Nothing about you, directly. The site:

  • sets no cookies;
  • uses no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts;
  • has no forms or accounts;
  • serves its fonts and images from this domain, not from third parties.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver pages and keep the service secure, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address and browser details. GitHub handles that data under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use those logs.

Links to the App Store

App Store badges take you to Apple. Anything you do there is covered by Apple’s privacy policy, not this one.

Support email

If you email us, we use your email address and what you write only to reply and to fix the problem you report. We keep support email for as long as needed to help you, and for no more than two years after the conversation ends. We do not sell it or use it for marketing.

Your rights

Where UK GDPR, EU GDPR or similar laws apply, you can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email support@catfordlabs.com and we will reply within one month. In the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children

This website is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
