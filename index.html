New · iPhone game

Locktone

Find. Lock. Fire.

A landscape arcade dogfighter. You sit in an open canopy with hostiles in the dark around you. Hold one in the reticle until the tone goes solid — then fire. Boost and flare when something settles on your six.

  • Daily seeded runs
  • Game Center leaderboards
  • No ads · no in-app purchases

SentryCam

Watch the room. Keep the clip.

Turn an iPhone or iPad you already own into a motion-triggered sentry camera. Arm it when you leave, and it records short clips — with context before and after — whenever something moves.

  • Pre-roll & post-roll clips
  • Clips on device or in iCloud
  • Apple TV companion
